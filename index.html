Robert Mahari

Boston, Massachusetts

About

Robert Mahari is the founder of Akiva, which empowers legal organizations to bring AI into the systems and practices they already rely on. Akiva supports every stage of AI transformation, combining custom AI development on clients' existing tools and data with AI-native advisory.

Previously, Robert served as Associate Director of Stanford's CodeX Center for Legal Informatics. He holds a joint JD-PhD from the MIT Media Lab and Harvard Law School, focused on legal AI, and serves on the advisory boards of several legal tech companies.

His research on legal AI has been published in venues including Science, Nature Machine Intelligence, and Nature Computational Science, with coverage in the New York Times and the Washington Post.
